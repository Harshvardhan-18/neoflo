Visual AI Agent — Privacy Disclosure

Please review how your browsing activity and visual data will be collected and processed before enabling tracking.

What is Collected

  • Browsing Events: Navigation URLs, click element tags & coarse CSS selectors, scroll depth milestones.
  • Visual Captures: Downscaled page screenshots processed server-side by Gemini 2.5 Flash Vision.
  • Session Identifiers: Anonymous per-install ID.

Privacy Safeguards

  • No Form Values: Input values, passwords, and autofill fields are NEVER captured.
  • Domain Blocklist: Financial, medical, and credential portals are skipped automatically.
  • Local-First Control: One-click pause and instant data purge from Settings.

Data Rights & Ownership

Your data belongs to you. You can inspect active blocklist rules, download a JSON export of your sessions, or execute a permanent data deletion at any time in the Extension Options Page.

Consent Required — Tracking is disabled by default.
